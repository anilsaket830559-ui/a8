Ricetta 01

How to Make Risotto at Home

Good risotto is creamy without cream. The starch from the rice, slow stirring and a generous finish of butter and cheese do all the work.

Serves
4
Time
35 min
Level
Easy
1

Choose the right rice

Use a short, plump Italian risotto rice such as Arborio, Carnaroli or Vialone Nano. These grains release starch as they cook, which thickens the stock into a sauce, yet hold their shape with a gentle bite at the centre. Do not rinse the rice; you want that starch.

✽
2

Keep the stock hot

Warm stock keeps the rice cooking evenly.

  • Use about 1 litre of good vegetable or chicken stock for 320 g of rice.
  • Keep it barely simmering in a pan next to the risotto.
  • Season lightly; the cheese at the end adds salt too.
3

Soften, toast, then add stock

Cook a finely chopped onion in butter or olive oil until soft and sweet. Add the rice and stir for a minute or two until the edges look glassy. Then add a ladle of hot stock and stir until it is almost absorbed before adding the next.

✽
4

Stir and taste

You do not need to stir constantly, but stir often, which rubs starch from the grains and keeps the rice from sticking. After about 15 minutes start tasting. The rice is ready when it is tender with a slight firmness in the middle, usually between 16 and 20 minutes.

✽
5

Finish all'onda

Take the pan off the heat. Add a final splash of stock, a knob of cold butter and a handful of finely grated cheese, and beat hard with a wooden spoon. Cover and rest for two minutes. Perfect risotto flows gently when the plate is tilted, like a slow wave.
