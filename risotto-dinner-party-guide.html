Ricetta 02

Planning a Risotto Dinner

Risotto has a reputation for keeping the cook at the stove. With a little planning you can serve it to friends and still enjoy the evening.

Serves
4
Time
35 min
Level
Easy
1

Plan the menu around the risotto

Risotto is rich and filling, so keep the rest of the meal light. A fresh salad or a few grilled vegetables to start, a simple dessert to finish, and perhaps a small second course if your guests have big appetites.

✽
2

Get everything ready first

Do the preparation before guests arrive.

  • Chop the onion and any vegetables or mushrooms.
  • Grate the cheese and cube the butter, then chill it.
  • Have the stock simmering and the bowls warming.
3

Use the restaurant trick

For larger groups, cook the risotto about two thirds of the way, around 12 minutes, then spread it on a tray to cool quickly and chill. When guests are seated, return it to the pan with hot stock and finish it in six to eight minutes.

✽
4

Bring guests into the kitchen

Risotto is a sociable dish. Invite friends to gather around the stove with a drink and a snack while you stir. The last ten minutes are the best moment of the evening, and nobody minds waiting for food they can watch being made.

✽
5

Serve it immediately

Risotto thickens as it stands, so serve the moment it is ready. Spoon it into warm, shallow bowls, tap each bowl so the risotto spreads, and add a final grating of cheese at the table. Leftovers make excellent crisp risotto cakes the next day.
